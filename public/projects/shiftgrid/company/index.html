COMPANY / PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The interface should
resemble the operation.

SHIFTGRID is a product concept built around a precise question: what changes when operational software understands physical space?

Read the platform architecture ↗
Physical work became
more connected.
Its software stayed
fragmented.

A warehouse does not end at its walls. Picking feeds staging. Staging feeds docks. Docks connect to the yard, and the yard connects to the road.

Yet the operator often has to assemble that picture from separate tables, dashboards and systems. SHIFTGRID imagines an interface that starts with the operation itself.

THE PHYSICAL OPERATION / MADE READABLE
PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Clarity has
a structure.

01

Space explains context.

Geometry is useful when it reveals where work happens and which movements depend on one another.

02

State explains movement.

A truck is more than a marker. It has a shipment, a destination, a dock assignment and a reason to move.

03

Decisions show their inputs.

Keep constraints and expected impact visible. Operators should understand a recommendation before acting on it.

04

Existing systems keep ownership.

Connect the operation without creating another ambiguous record of inventory, commercial context or transport plans.

A fictional product and design engineering project. No real customers, offices, funding rounds, staff biographies or production deployments are represented.
